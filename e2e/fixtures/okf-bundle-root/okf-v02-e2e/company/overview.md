## About the company

Ridgeway Commercial Services Ltd is a UK-based distributor of office supplies, furniture and light facilities services. Its customer base is drawn from UK public-sector buyers, including local authorities, NHS trusts and education trusts.

## Scale and reach

The company employs approximately 180 people. Operations are run from four regional fulfilment hubs, with warehousing, logistics and delivery performance against agreed key performance indicators (KPIs) overseen by the Operations Director.

## Leadership and team structure

Ridgeway is led by **Naomi Whitfield**, Managing Director, who holds overall responsibility for strategy, bid governance and buyer relationships. She is supported by four functional leads:

- **Callum Rees**, Head of Compliance and Quality — owns the company's [ISO 9001:2015](/certifications/iso-9001.md) certification, [Facilities Assurance Standard (FAS 2100)](/certifications/facilities-assurance-standard-fas-2100.md) accreditation and [Sustainable Supply Chain Mark (SSCM)](/certifications/sustainable-supply-chain-mark-sscm.md), and chairs the quarterly compliance review.
- **Priya Anand**, Head of Account Management — leads the team of named account managers assigned to key accounts, including current engagements with Wyndale Metropolitan Borough Council, St Aldhelm's NHS Foundation Trust and Corvedale Academies Trust.
- **Daniel Osei**, Operations Director — responsible for the four regional fulfilment hubs, warehousing, logistics and delivery performance against agreed KPIs.
- **Grace Fenton**, Head of Sustainability and Social Value — oversees the [GreenCycle Furniture Programme](/products/greencycle-furniture-programme.md) and the company's broader sustainability and social-value commitments.

## Certifications and accreditations

Ridgeway holds three principal accreditations:

- [ISO 9001:2015](/certifications/iso-9001.md) — quality management system certification.
- [Facilities Assurance Standard (FAS 2100)](/certifications/facilities-assurance-standard-fas-2100.md) — facilities assurance accreditation.
- [Sustainable Supply Chain Mark (SSCM)](/certifications/sustainable-supply-chain-mark-sscm.md) — sustainable supply chain certification.

All three are held by Ridgeway Commercial Services Ltd in the capacity of supplier, and ownership of certification maintenance sits with the Head of Compliance and Quality.
