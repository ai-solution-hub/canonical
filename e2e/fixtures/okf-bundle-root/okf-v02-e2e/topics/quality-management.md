Quality management at Ridgeway Commercial Services Ltd is structured around an externally audited ISO 9001:2015 quality-management system. Certification is maintained on a recurring cycle: the organisation undergoes annual surveillance audits, with a full recertification every three years to keep the certification current.

The system is owned by the Head of Compliance and Quality, who is accountable for the documented procedures that fall within its scope. The principal procedures cover order accuracy, returns, and complaint handling — these are the operational touchpoints where the quality-management system is most visible to customers and where conformity is monitored and corrected.

Because the framework is both externally certified and internally documented, it provides a formal assurance loop: certification audit confirms the system's design and currency, while the documented procedures provide the day-to-day mechanism for delivering accurate orders, processing returns, and resolving complaints. This positions quality management as a governance discipline rather than a one-off accreditation, with clear ownership and recurring review.

For details of the underlying certification, see [ISO 9001](/certifications/iso-9001.md).
