## Overview

Ridgeway Commercial Services Ltd processes personal data relating to both buyers and staff under documented data-processing agreements that are aligned to the UK GDPR. The company maintains formal breach-handling procedures, and ownership for data-protection compliance sits with the [Compliance and Quality department](/topics/quality-management.md), which is led by Callum Rees, Head of Compliance and Quality.

## Scope of personal data

Ridgeway's data-protection controls cover two principal categories of personal data:

- **Buyer information** — personal data supplied by or about customers in the course of commercial engagements.
- **Staff information** — personal data relating to Ridgeway's own workforce.

Both categories are processed under the same governance framework: documented data-processing agreements aligned to UK GDPR requirements.

## Governance and accountability

Day-to-day responsibility for data protection rests with the Compliance and Quality department. The designated point of contact for data-protection queries is Callum Rees, Head of Compliance and Quality, who routes and oversees all such enquiries through that department.

This placement ties data-protection oversight to Ridgeway's broader [quality management](/topics/quality-management.md) function, ensuring that compliance, assurance and data-handling controls are managed coherently rather than as a standalone activity.

## Breach handling

Ridgeway maintains formal breach-handling procedures as part of its data-protection framework. These procedures are owned by the Compliance and Quality department, providing a defined escalation and response route should a personal-data breach occur.

## Relationship to information-security certification

While data-protection governance is owned by Compliance and Quality, Ridgeway's information-security posture is further supported through its [ISO 27001](/certifications/iso-27001.md) certification, which provides the management-system context in which personal data is protected technically and operationally.

## Summary

In short, Ridgeway's approach to data protection combines three elements: UK-GDPR-aligned data-processing agreements covering buyer and staff data, formally owned breach-handling procedures, and a clear accountable contact — the Compliance and Quality department under Callum Rees — for all data-protection queries.
